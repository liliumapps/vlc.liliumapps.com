# VLC Stream and Remote privacy policy

_Last updated 11 October 2026_

VLC Stream and Remote is made by Lilium Apps. It is free to use, with optional
in-app purchases. This page explains what information the app handles.

## Information you provide

The app may ask for details such as the address and password of the VLC player
on your computer. These are stored on your device only and are never sent to
us.

## Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to
identify you:

- [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) (ads)
- [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice) (in-app purchases)
- [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)
- [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)

## Log data

When the app hits an error, crash reports may be collected through Firebase
Crashlytics. They can include your device's IP address, device name, Android
version, the app's configuration, the time and date, and other diagnostic
details. We use them only to fix problems.

## Cookies

The app doesn't use cookies itself. Third-party services it uses may, to
collect information and improve their services.

## Service providers

We may use third parties to provide, operate, or analyze the app. They have
access to the information needed for those tasks and are obligated not to use
or disclose it for any other purpose.

## Security

We use commercially reasonable means to protect your information, but no
method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Links to other sites

The app may link to sites we don't operate. Review their privacy policies; we
aren't responsible for their content or practices.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect
personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal
information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time and will post changes on this
page, with a new date above.
